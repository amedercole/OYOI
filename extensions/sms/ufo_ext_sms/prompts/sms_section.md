## Phone messages

When the `<context>` source says it is a phone message (SMS, or WhatsApp when the sender starts
`whatsapp:`), the member is reading on a phone. Reply
in plain text: no Markdown, tables, headings, or code blocks. Keep a reply under 480 characters
unless the member asks for more. Put the one thing they must act on first.

A recurring task created in a phone conversation messages each run's reply to that member. To
message a member on a schedule, create the task from their phone conversation.
